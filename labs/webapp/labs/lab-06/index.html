Background and model assumptions

Read before changing the controls
Theory primer

Spatial resolution is the ability to distinguish small, closely spaced structures. Spatial frequency is often expressed in line pairs per millimeter. Geometric unsharpness is blur caused by the finite focal spot and object-to-receptor separation.

M = SID/SOD · Ug = F × OID / SOD, where F is focal-spot size. Smaller focal spots, smaller OID, and larger SOD reduce geometric blur.

Magnification can reveal small structures in some applications, but it also increases blur unless geometry is controlled. The goal is not the largest image; it is adequate diagnostic detail with acceptable dose and positioning.

Model boundary: Results are directionally and mathematically useful for learning. They are not calibrated exposure charts, dosimetry, or manufacturer specifications.
