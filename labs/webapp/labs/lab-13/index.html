Background and model assumptions

Theory primer

The IMAGE mnemonic organizes radiographic recording and analysis into a rigorous, systematic clinical pathway:

  • I — Initial Patient & System Setup: Confirm two unique patient identifiers (Name & DOB/MRN), check requisition/indication, choose the receptor format (Tabletop DR flat-panel for knee <10 cm vs. Table Bucky 8:1 Grid for thicker parts), and set standard 100 cm (40 in) SID.
  • M — Manual Selection of Exposure Factors: Set kVp to control X-ray beam penetration and subject contrast (68–72 kVp baseline for knee). Set mAs for adequate photon fluence without quantum mottle (target 200 EI). Choose Small Focal Spot (0.6 mm) for high-detail bone trabeculae.
  • A — Anatomy Positioning and Preparation: Position the knee in True AP (femoral epicondyles parallel to IR, tibial plateau open, proximal fibula ⅓ superimposed). Eliminate radiopaque external artifacts (zippers, snaps, knee braces). Support limb with sponges/sandbags to prevent involuntary motion.
  • G — Guide the Beam and Patient: Center Central Ray (CR) 1.25 cm (0.5 in) distal to patellar apex through the joint space. Tightly collimate to anatomical margins (~18×24 cm) to reduce scatter and protect patient gonads. Place lead side marker ('R') laterally within the primary beam outside the VOI. Give clear "hold still" instructions.
  • E — Evaluate the Image: Systematic critique of diagnostic criteria and ALARA review (covered in Lab 14).
Exposure Maintenance / Inverse Square Law: mAs2 = mAs1 × (SID2 ⁄ SID1)2.
Receptor Exposure Index & Deviation Index (DI): EI ∝ mAs × (kVp ⁄ 70)2.2 × (100 ⁄ SID)2 × GridFactor. DI = 10 · log10(EI ⁄ EIT). Target range: −1.0 to +1.0 DI.
Geometric & Total Unsharpness: Ug = FSS × OID⁄SOD; Utotal = √(Ug2 + Umotion2) in mm.
Collimation Scatter Fraction (S/P): S/P ∝ Field Area (cm2)⁄432 × (kVp ⁄ 70)0.8. Tight collimation drastically enhances subject contrast.
IMAGE Composite Readiness: Score = 0.20·SI + 0.25·SM + 0.25·SA + 0.30·SG with critical gating (missing marker ≤55%, metal artifact ≤45%, unverified ID ≤45%, clipped anatomy ≤48%).

Model boundary: Results are directionally and mathematically accurate for learning clinical radiograph acquisition and the IMAGE method. They are not calibrated equipment exposure charts.
