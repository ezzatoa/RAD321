Background and model assumptions

Theory primer

Optical density (OD) describes film blackening: OD = log₁₀(I₀ / Iₜ), where I₀ is incident light and Iₜ is transmitted light. Higher OD means less light passes through the processed film. A characteristic curve plots OD against the logarithm of exposure.

Toe: low-density region with limited separation · Straight line: useful exposure range · Shoulder: saturation region · Gamma: slope of the straight-line portion.

Film speed is the exposure needed to reach a reference density; latitude is the useful exposure range. A steeper curve has higher contrast but narrower latitude.

Model boundary: Results are directionally and mathematically useful for learning. They are not calibrated exposure charts, dosimetry, or manufacturer specifications.
