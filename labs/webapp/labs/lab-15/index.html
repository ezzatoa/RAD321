Theory primer

An artifact is any unwanted visual density or feature on a radiograph that does not represent the patient’s true anatomical structures. In modern diagnostic imaging, artifact analysis is a vital quality-assurance competency:

  • 1. Modern Digital Radiography (DR Flat-Panel) Artifacts: Defective TFT detector pixel lines/columns (hardware failure requiring service calibration), ghosting / image lag from prior high-dose exposures, RF electronic noise banding, and flat-field calibration failure.
  • 2. Computed Radiography (CR / PSP) Artifacts: Incomplete optical erasure (residual latent image ghosting from unreleased trapped electrons), dirty light-guide / reader optics (fine white scan line), and Moiré pattern (spatial frequency aliasing between stationary grid frequency and laser scan sampling frequency near the Nyquist limit).
  • 3. Exposure & Technique Artifacts: Quantum mottle (severe photon starvation from sub-threshold mAs), focused grid cutoff (severe lateral density loss and noise from off-level or off-center beam alignment), and double exposures.
  • 4. Patient Preparation & Motion Artifacts: Unremoved radiopaque foreign objects (zippers, snaps, jewelry, buttons) obscuring the diagnostic VOI, and involuntary/voluntary patient motion causing kinetic penumbra unsharpness.
  • 5. Screen-Film & Darkroom Artifacts: Static discharge arborization (tree, crown, smudge marks from friction in low humidity <40%), and automatic processor pi lines (\(\pi \times d\) periodic roller deposits) and guide-shoe scratches.
IMAGE Root-Cause Tracing Method: Stage I (Plate erasure, IR selection) → Stage M (Photon fluence, mAs) → Stage A (Gowning, immobilization) → Stage G (CR centering, grid alignment) → Stage E (TFT line, processor rollers).
ALARA Repeat Directive: Re-exposure is strictly prohibited if diagnostic integrity within the Volume of Interest (VOI) is preserved or recoverable by digital post-processing/windowing.
Defective Hardware Protocol: For fixed detector hardware faults, repeating exposures on the patient is clinically negligent; the receptor must be immediately quarantined from service.

Model boundary: Visualizations and mathematical scores are directionally and clinically accurate for teaching systematic artifact root-cause analysis and ALARA gating.
